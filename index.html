01

Authority

This system is the source of truth. When anything disagrees with it, this system wins. It replaces StrixMCS-Branding and StrixHA as the design and branding standard.

The conceit is an ancient posture and current execution: a centuries-old seal rendered in clean modern monospace. Lean into the age; refuse the costume. The standard applies whether or not anyone else will see the artifact.

02

Entities

The Order of the Strix is the umbrella: the society, its seal, motto and ceremony. StrixMCS is the house, and what runs at home stays there. StrixMCS Labs is the press, and what leaves the house is published there.

03

The seal

One mark: the owl perched on the open book, white line-art on absolute black. It is both the working mark and the regalia. StrixMCS Labs carries the same mark, whole; the lockup and the byline mark Labs work.

  • Keep it intact. Never crop, separate, recolor, rotate, glow or bevel it. Never use the owl head alone.
  • Place it on black. Absolute black only, with clear space on all sides equal to the owl-head height.
  • Give it size. Do not shrink it to a cramped favicon.

Files: 256, 512, 1024, full resolution and the transparent owl.

04

Voice

Serious, precise, service-oriented and documentation-minded. Plain verbs over jargon. Dry wit is allowed where it never costs clarity.

  • Normal case everywhere. The Order does not shout.
  • Third person or imperative. It documents systems. It does not address the reader.
  • No emoji. Ever.
  • Credit. Work built on another project names that project and its authors directly beneath the byline, and never implies endorsement.

05

Color

The field is black, everywhere. Three colors are held apart, each with exactly one job. No pastels, no gradients, no imagery and no texture.

06

Type

Mono leads. IBM Plex Mono carries titles, labels, the wordmark, the motto and code. IBM Plex Sans carries running prose at 15–16px on 1.6 leading. Weight and size carry hierarchy; bold is never decoration.

07

Space

A quiet 4px grid with generous margins. Depth comes from the hairline ring, not the drop shadow. Motion is measured, with no bounce: the Order does not spring.

10

Use

Source: strixmcs-labs/StrixMCS-Design. Clone the repository and link styles.css, which imports the token and font-face files. Components mount from the bundled window namespace.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="styles.css">
<script src="_ds_bundle.js"></script>

const { Seal, Wordmark, Motto } = window.StrixMCSOrderDesignSystem_72a56f;

The full specification is in readme.md. Agents start at SKILL.md.

UI icons use Lucide as a stand-in for Home Assistant's Material Design Icons, at a 1.6px line weight. Projects that need exact Home Assistant parity swap in MDI and keep the weight.
