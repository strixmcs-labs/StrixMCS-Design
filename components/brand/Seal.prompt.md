# Seal

The single mark of the Order — owl over book, white line-art on absolute black. The working mark and the regalia both. Give it size; keep it on black.

```jsx
<Seal size={260} src="assets/StrixMCS-seal-512.png" />
```

- **src** must point to a copied seal asset — copy one of `assets/StrixMCS-seal-*.png` into the consuming project and pass its path.
- Keep the owl+book intact; never recolor, rotate, crop, or place on anything but black. **clearspace** reserves the required margin.
