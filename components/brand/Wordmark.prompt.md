# Wordmark

The wordmark, set immutably: **StrixMCS** — capital S, lowercase, MCS in capitals, always mono and normal case. Never ~~STRIXMCS~~, ~~strixmcs~~, or ~~Strix MCS~~.

```jsx
<Wordmark size={38} />
<Wordmark size={28} tagline />
```

- **entity**: `"house"` (default, StrixMCS) or `"labs"` (StrixMCS Labs lockup). `"order"` and `labs` are deprecated aliases.
- **tagline**: adds "The Order of the Strix" beneath. **size**: px. **color**: stay within the palette (white default).
