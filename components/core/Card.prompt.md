# Card

A raised near-black surface on the black field, ringed by a hairline. The default container for panels, tiles, and content blocks.

```jsx
<Card>Living-room occupancy holds through stillness.</Card>
<Card variant="ceremonial" padding="lg">The motto, framed.</Card>
<Card variant="code">$ ha core restart</Card>
```

- **variant**: `default`, `raised` (elevated), `ceremonial` (oxblood inner glow — formal only), `code` (terminal surface, mono).
- **accentLeft**: adds a 3px oxblood left rule. **padding**: `none`/`sm`/`md`/`lg`.
